# ZYEP Brand Identity Kit
## Services. On Demand.

This document serves as the official design system and brand guidelines for **ZYEP**. All UI development (Angular) and backend assets (CodeIgniter 4) should strictly adhere to these specifications to ensure brand consistency and user trust.

---

## 1. Visual Identity & Logo
The ZYEP logo represents speed, reliability, and community enrichment.

* **Primary Symbol:** The 'Z-Bolt' (A Navy Blue 'Z' base with a Bright Orange Lightning Bolt overlay).
* **Logotype:** Bold, sans-serif typography (Inter/Montserrat style).
* **Brand Slogan:** "Services. On Demand."
* **Marketing Hook:** "Don't just search for it—ZYEP it."

---

---

## 3. Typography
**Primary Font:** [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) (Google Fonts)
* **Headings (H1, H2):** SemiBold (600) or Bold (700). Use Uppercase for ZYEP branding.
* **Body Text:** Regular (400).
* **Interactive Elements:** Medium (500) for buttons and navigation labels.

--- 

## 4. UI Components & Design Language
### Buttons (The "Call to Action")
* **Primary Action (Call/Book):** Background: `Accent Orange`, Text: `White`.
* **Secondary Action (Profile/View):** Background: `White`, Border: `1px solid Primary Navy`, Text: `Primary Navy`.
* **Border Radius:** 12px (Smooth rounded corners).

### Cards & Elevation
* **Provider Cards:** White background with a subtle shadow.
* **Shadow:** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06);`

### Iconography
* **Style:** Clean, thin-line icons (e.g., Lucide or HeroIcons).
* **Container:** Icons should be placed in a light-grey circular background (`#F3F4F6`) for category grids.

---

## 5. Brand Voice
* **Tone:** Efficient, Local, Empowering, and Reliable.
* **Terminology:** * Use "Provider" or "Professional" rather than "Worker."
    * Use "Instant Connection" instead of "Wait for call-back."
